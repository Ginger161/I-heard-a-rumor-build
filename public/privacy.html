What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, scores and saved game are stored on your own device (browser storage). We do not have accounts, and we do not see this data.

What we may collect

  • Visit counts, if analytics is switched on: cookie-free, no personal data.
  • Daily reminders, only if you turn them on: our push provider (OneSignal) receives a device token, and facts such as your streak, so we can send reminders. Turn them off in Settings at any time.
  • Waitlist email, only if you type one in: used only to tell you when the Gist Pass opens.
  • Payments, when they open: handled by our payment provider (for example Paystack). We never see your card details.

Scores you share

When you share a score link, it contains the name and crew you chose, and your result. Share only what you are happy for others to see.
